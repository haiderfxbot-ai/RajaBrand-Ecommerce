Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?expand_more

Simply browse our products, add items to cart, and proceed to checkout. You can place an order as a guest or sign up for a faster experience.

What payment methods do you accept?expand_more

We offer Cash on Delivery (COD) across all major cities in Pakistan. Pay when your order arrives at your doorstep.

How long does delivery take?expand_more

Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days. Metro cities like Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad usually get delivery within 2-4 days.

What is your return policy?expand_more

We offer 7-day easy returns from the date of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Contact support to initiate a return.
