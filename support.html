Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?expand_more

Simply browse our products, add items to cart, and proceed to checkout. No account needed — you can check out as a guest!

What payment methods do you accept?expand_more

We currently offer Cash on Delivery (COD) for all orders. Pay when your order arrives.

How long does delivery take?expand_more

Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days depending on your location. Metro cities usually get delivery within 2-4 days.

Is delivery free?expand_more

Yes! We offer free delivery on all orders above ₹499. A small ₹49 fee applies for orders below this amount.

What is the return policy?expand_more

We offer 7-day easy returns from the date of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

How do I track my order?expand_more

Go to "My Orders" in your profile and click "Track Order" to see real-time updates on your delivery status.

Can I cancel my order?expand_more

Yes, you can cancel your order before it is dispatched. Once dispatched, please contact support for assistance.

How do I apply a coupon?expand_more

Enter your coupon code in the "Have a coupon?" field on the cart page and click "Apply". The discount will be reflected in your order summary.

Is my personal information safe?expand_more

Absolutely! We take your privacy seriously. Your data is stored securely and never shared with third parties.

How do I contact customer support?expand_more

You can call us, WhatsApp us, or email us using the options at the top of this page. We're here 24/7!

Policies

We offer a full refund on items returned within 7 days of delivery. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. Items must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging. COD charges are non-refundable.

Raja Brand respects your privacy. We collect only necessary information (name, email, phone, address) to process your orders. Your data is encrypted and never shared with third parties. You can request data deletion at any time by contacting support.

By using Raja Brand, you agree to provide accurate information, not misuse the platform, and comply with all applicable laws. We reserve the right to cancel orders if fraudulent activity is suspected. Prices and availability are subject to change without notice.
